## 🔥 Penetration Testing Simulation
Simulated real-world attack lifecycle in a controlled environment using Kali Linux.

- **Reconnaissance:** Nmap scans to identify open ports and services  
- **Exploitation:** Used Metasploit to gain initial access  
- **Post-Exploitation:** Extracted user data, escalated privileges  
- **Reporting:** Delivered findings with remediation steps (patch management, least privilege)

🔗 Tools: Kali Linux, Metasploit, Nmap  
🏷 MITRE ATT&CK: T1046 (Network Scanning), T1059 (Command & Scripting), T1069 (Permission Groups)
